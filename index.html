What Stet does about it

Checks on your laptop

The model built into Chrome reads your text on your own computer. A counter at the top shows what has left it. For a normal check, that number stays 0.

One message for your agent

You do not fix anything by hand. Stet writes the new text and one message that tells your agent exactly what to change. You paste it.

The cloud only where it helps

A cloud model writes your wording guide from two lines you type, and one standing rule for your agent. It never needs your app's text for that.

How to use it

  1. Scan with StetMailDocs

    1. Add the bookmark

    Drag the "Scan with Stet" button to your bookmarks bar. You do this once.

  2. Submit

    2. Click it on your app

    Open your app on the screen you want checked, on localhost or online, and click the bookmark.

  3. Copy fix

    3. Paste the fix into your agent

    Stet lists what to fix. Press "Copy fix for my AI agent" and paste it into Claude Code, Cursor, Lovable or whatever built your app.

The example is a small invoicing app with mistakes planted in it. One click scans it, with no bookmark needed.

What to expect

  • Made for app screens. Buttons, labels, field hints, error and empty messages, menus. On a marketing page it still finds real defects, but it flags much plain copy too.
  • About 3 seconds a piece of text. A screen with 20 pieces takes about a minute the first time, and is instant when you check it again.
  • Desktop Chrome. The model built into Chrome does the checking. Without it, Stet can use a cloud model, and only after showing you exactly what it would send.
  • You decide. Every finding has a tick box. Untick what you disagree with before you copy the fix.

If it says the model is not available

Chrome desktop 139 or newer can run it; it needs about 22 GB of free disk space. In Chrome, turn on chrome://flags/#prompt-api-for-gemini-nano-multimodal-input, restart Chrome, then download the model here.

Without it you can still check text: untick "Keep my text on this laptop", and Stet shows you the exact text first and sends it to the cloud model only after you agree.
