What runs on your laptop, and what runs in the cloud

The model in Chrome checks your text

It runs on your own computer. A counter at the top shows what has left your laptop. When you keep your text on the laptop, it stays at 0.

One fix message for your agent

Stet writes the new text and one fix message that names each piece of text and what to change it to. You paste it into your coding agent.

The cloud model writes your wording guide

It works from two lines you type about your app, and from the count of each kind of problem. Your app's text is not sent for either.

How to use it: three steps

  1. Scan with StetMailDocs

    1. Add the bookmark

    Drag the "Scan with Stet" button to your bookmarks bar. You do this once.

  2. Submit

    2. Click it on your app

    Open your app on the screen you want checked, on localhost or online, and click the bookmark.

  3. Copy fix

    3. Paste the fix into your agent

    Stet lists what to fix. Press "Copy fix for my coding agent" and paste it into Claude Code, Cursor, Lovable or the agent that built your app.

Check my app Try it on the example app

The example app is a small invoicing screen with 8 mistakes planted in it. It needs no bookmark.

What to expect

  • Made for app screens. Buttons, labels, field hints, error and empty messages, menus. On a marketing page it still finds real defects, but it flags much plain copy too.
  • About 3 seconds a piece of text. The example screen has 22 pieces and took 56 seconds the first time. Checking the same text again takes under a second.
  • Desktop Chrome. The model built into Chrome does the checking. Without it, Stet can use a cloud model, and only after showing you exactly what it would send.
  • You decide. Every finding has a tick box. Untick what you disagree with before you copy the fix.

If it says the model is not available

Chrome desktop 139 or newer can run it; it needs about 22 GB of free disk space. In Chrome, turn on chrome://flags/#prompt-api-for-gemini-nano-multimodal-input, restart Chrome, then download the model here.

Without it you can still check text: untick "Keep my text on this laptop", and Stet shows you the exact text first and sends it to the cloud model only after you agree.
