Check one screen of your app.

Stet reads the text on one screen, marks the pieces that need work, and writes one fix message for your coding agent.

Made for app screens: buttons, labels, field hints, messages and menus. On a marketing page it still finds real defects, but flags much plain copy too.

1. Get your screen's text

Drag me up to your bookmarks bar Scan with Stet No bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B. You only do this once.
  1. Drag the blue button to your bookmarks bar.
  2. Open your app in another Chrome tab, on the screen you want checked.
  3. Click "Scan with Stet" in the bookmarks bar. The text of that screen opens here. It goes from your app's tab straight to this tab. After the check, the numbered marks go back to that tab and are drawn on your screen, where your app's own scripts can also see them.
