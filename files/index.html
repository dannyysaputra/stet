Check your app's text

Stet reads the words on one screen of your app, checks them on this laptop, and gives you one message to paste into your AI coding agent. Made for app screens: buttons, labels, field hints, messages and menus. On a marketing page it still finds real defects, but flags much plain copy too.

1. Get your screen's text

Scan with Stet
  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar. You do this once. (No bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B.)
  2. Open your app in another Chrome tab, on the screen you want checked.
  3. Click "Scan with Stet" in the bookmarks bar. The text of that screen opens here. It goes from your app's tab straight to this tab, and nowhere else.

Not sure it worked? Test your bookmark on our example app, or try the example without a bookmark.
